Model Gallery Label

Fanhuang Bamboo Carving
Huangyan, Taizhou, Zhejiang · national intangible cultural heritage (2008)黄岩翻簧竹雕
artist / origin→background→features→value
Image: illustration of bamboo carving from the course slides (not a specific fanhuang work).
This delicate carving comes from Huangyan, Taizhou, where fanhuang bamboo carving began in the Qing Dynasty. Craftsmen peel the thin inner layer of bamboo, boil and flatten it, glue it onto wood, then carve landscapes and figures on a surface under half a millimetre thick. Listed as national intangible cultural heritage in 2008, it shows how humble bamboo becomes elegant art.

Why this label works
✓ about 60 words   ✓ facts first, feelings last   ✓ unit adjectives: delicate, elegant
✓ one clear process (peel → boil → flatten → glue → carve)   ✓ ends with the value of the art
Facts checked with the Huangyan Museum, the National Library of China ICH archive and Guangming Daily. Master Luo Qisong (b. 1935) is the national representative inheritor of this craft.
